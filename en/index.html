Your progress and created files are automatically saved to your browser cache. Even if you close the page, you can pick up exactly where you left off.

📚 Curriculum & Lesson Selection

Click the ☰ (Menu) button in the top left corner to access the full curriculum, see completed or active chapters, and quickly switch to any lesson.

📖 Training Book (PDF)

You can access the official "The Linux Command Line" book, which forms the basis of this training, directly from the link at the very top of the ☰ (Curriculum) menu, and practice in the terminal while reading it.

💾 Backing Up Progress (Backup)

If you are switching to another browser or computer, or just want to take a precaution, use the "Backup" button to download your progress as a tlcl_backup_en.json file to your computer.

📂 Restoring Backup (Restore)

Select your previously downloaded backup with the "Restore" button to load it into the system and continue exactly where you left off.

🔄 Resetting Training

If you want to start from the absolute beginning, use the "Reset" button. This action completely deletes all your progress and saved files.

📜 Sample Scripts

Actual script examples mentioned in the book are pre-loaded in the /home/user/scripts/ directory. You can try running these scripts with bash scripts/filename.
